Junior Jazz (Ages 5-11)

Jazz is an energetic and expressive dance style which combines technique, musicality and performance. Classes will include a mixture of warm-ups, technical exercises, travelling sequences and choreography helping dancers to develop their confidence, coordination, flexibility and performance skills.

Junior Commercial (Ages 5-11)

Commercial is a fun, high energy dance style inspired by the choreography you might see in music videos, live performances and on stage. Classes focus on learning exiting choreography whilst developing your musicality, confidence, performance quality and individual style. Commercial is perfect for dancers who love performing and want to learn choreography with a fresh, modern feel whilst expressing themselves through movement.
