Welcome

Welcome to Ever Rising Dance - created from a genuine love of dance. My vision is to create a welcoming space where every dancer can discover confidence, develop their skills, make new friendships and most importantly share the love of dance. Whether you are an absolute beginner with no dance experience previously or want to develop your skills further, everyone is welcome!
